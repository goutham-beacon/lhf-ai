High-claim vehicles: what to do

Some models get a damage claim 10 to 17 times more often than average. Watch the 60-second video, then use the five steps every time.

1

Inspect before you touch it

Walk around. Take 8 photos: front, rear, both sides, all four corners. Mark every scratch and chip on the inspection report.

Not marked at pickup = it is ours.
2

Low cars: load slow

Use the ramp extensions. Check the front lip clearance. Roll at walking speed.

Hear a scrape? Stop. Reset the ramp.
3

Big trucks and vans: check the fit

Measure the height before the top deck. Fold the mirrors. Watch the dually fenders and the steps.

Not sure it fits? Put it on the bottom.
4

Secure it, then check again

Tie down to the frame points. Keep the straps off the paint and the suspension.

Re-check all straps after the first 50 km.
5

At delivery

Walk around with the receiver. Take the same 8 photos. Get the signature before you leave.

Photos at both ends close most claims.
